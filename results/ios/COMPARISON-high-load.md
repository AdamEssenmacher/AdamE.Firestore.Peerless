# iOS: peered vs Peerless (generated)

Generated by `scripts/compare-ios.py` from 4 runs. Every cell is a median over all callbacks of that case in all runs of that mode. Wall and CPU span the whole path each mode has: peered from entry into the .NET listener; Peerless from entry into the Objective-C listener (so it includes encoding). Peered ends with the snapshot converted into plain .NET values, Peerless with it decoded into the Peerless model. As on Android, *update* rows leave out the updates straight after a settle point, which are listed as *update, after a settle*. Alloc in KiB.
